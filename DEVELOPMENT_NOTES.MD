# Development Notes

- I wanted to do this project because I really like cybersecurity and cryptography. I got into them from reading books about the code breakers in World War II and then I just kept going. My grandma gets a ton of scam calls and spam emails and she actually got tricked by a fake Amazon message once, so I wanted to build something that could help people like her stay safe.

- For school I have to do community service hours, but I didn’t want to just do something random. I thought it would be way more useful to help older people in the community learn about cybersecurity because they’re the ones who get targeted the most. I’m also sharing everything I make with my grandma so she can learn it too.

- Before building anything, I did a lot of research on the most common problems. I used sites like AARP and a bunch of other places online that had stats about how much money and time people lose because of scams. It was honestly kind of shocking. That’s why I made the presentation in slow sections, so seniors can ask questions and not feel rushed.

- i built the first version of the site in september and october with MkDocs because it’s Python based and I was learning Python at the time. i used the agent in VS Studio to help me build the structure because I didn’t really know how to make a whole site from scratch yet.

- After I got all the content written, I wanted the site to look better and be easier to use. I asked my friend Vedansh to help because he’s good at HTML and design stuff. He rebuilt the whole front end in HTML/CSS so it looked more modern and not like a default template.

- We’re now talking with a couple of local retirement homes and libraries to run the presentations in person. The goal is to make the information simple enough that people don’t feel embarrassed asking questions, and to help them avoid the scams that keep happening to them.

- I also went on to build a phishing simulator at the same time because i wanted to give people examples of the kind of emails that can trick people. that was a fun project and challenging because i also had to learn how to use ai and even discovered cloudflare workers.
